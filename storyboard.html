⭐ Hero Shots — 7 Anchors of the Edit

These are the shots worth waking up early for. Everything else supports them. If you only get these seven, the film works.

Hero 1

Mavic Mini 3 Pro · 4K 60fps · HDR

Hero 2
Puffing Billy Steam Burst — 90mm Compression
S9 + 90mm APO · position 80–100m back on the track curve · steam explosion as locomotive accelerates · D6 Yarra Valley
S9 + 90mm APO f/2 · 4K 25fps
Arrive early to find the curve. The 90mm compresses background trees into a dark mass — steam reads brilliant white against it. Shoot the departure, not the approach.
Hero 3

S9 + Noctilux f/1.2 · tripod · 4K 25fps
The Leica Glow at f/1.2 on city lights is your visual signature. No other travel vlogger has this look. Window: 17:30–18:20. Bring GorillaPod or small tripod.
Hero 4
Brighton Beach Bathing Boxes — Sunrise Walk
S9 + 35mm Summilux · walk parallel to boxes at sunrise · long low shadows between boxes · arrive 06:45 · D1 or D4 morning
S9 + 35mm Summilux f/1.4 · 4K 25fpsDrone · colour grid aerial
Drone gets the iconic colour grid from above. S9 walks at box-level for cinematic ground perspective. Combine both angles in the edit.
Hero 5
Great Ocean Road Drive — Insta360 Cockpit
Insta360 X5 · suction mount on dashboard · full 360° coastal road all day · reframe in post to dramatic widescreen · D3
Insta360 X5 · dashboard mount · 5.7K all day

Hero 6
Kids' Reaction — Melbourne Zoo Enclosure
S9 + 35mm · shoot the faces, not the animals · gorilla or lion walkway · hold on the expression · D2
S9 + 35mm Summilux f/1.4

Hero 7

NO CAMERA ALLOWED after sunset · capture the anticipation instead: coastline at dusk, crowd gathering, last light over the Southern Ocean
S9 before sunset only · pack away before parade
"Some moments you just have to be there." This becomes the most honest shot in the film. In the edit: anticipation shots → cut to black → title card → next morning. The darkness holds the penguins.

🧰 Shot Toolkit — Every Location Needs These

At every new location, run through this sequence before moving on. Three shot types minimum = something editable in post.

Establish · Wide
  • Show where we are
  • Include something moving
  • Hold for 8+ seconds
B-roll · Medium
  • The activity happening
  • People in the environment
  • Don't announce the shot
Detail · Close
  • Food, textures, signs, hands
  • Context anchors for the viewer
  • f/1.4 or f/1.2 · soft background
Movement
  • Walk-with: chest height, IBIS on
  • Reveal: start tight, pull back
  • Lock-off: let subject move through
Reaction
  • Faces — not posed
  • Wait for the real expression
  • Kids over adults for authenticity
Ambient Audio
  • 60 sec stationary at each location
  • S9 on ground or railing
  • Don't talk during this take

⚙️ Lumix S9 — Settings to Lock Before Departure

Video Settings
Format4K 25fps — cinematic international standard
Slow-mo4K 60fps — steam burst, ocean waves, key moments
ProfileV-Log L for grading, or Leica Look LUT for quick delivery
IBISBody stabilisation ON at all times
AF ModeC-AF + Face/Eye detection for family shots
With Leica M Lenses via Adapter
FocusManual only — no AF through M-to-L adapter
Focus PeakingON · high sensitivity · red highlight colour
MF AssistON — magnify on focus ring turn
Noctilux f/1.2Focus on eyes. DOF at f/1.2 is ~3cm at 1m distance.
Leica GlowShoot wide-open. The spherical aberration IS the look.
Card speed: 4K 60fps requires V90 rated SD cards minimum. Slower cards drop frames mid-clip. Label your cards by day before leaving — swap at the hotel each evening and back up overnight.

✅ Pre-Trip Checklist

Gear
  • Format all SD cards — fresh start for the trip
  • 4× Lumix S9 batteries — ~300 shots each at 4K
  • 2× Mavic Mini 3 Pro batteries — 34 min each
  • Insta360 X5 batteries + both mounts — suction cup + dash clip
  • Joby GorillaPod or small tripod — blue hour + Puffing Billy lock-off
  • DJI Mic 2 or Rode VideoMicro II — optional but improves ambience capture
  • V90 cards × 4 minimum — 4K 60fps fills fast
  • M-to-L adapter — all three Leica M lenses on S9
Admin
  • Register Mavic Mini 3 Pro at casa.gov.au before departure
  • Download CASA app — check no-fly zone each morning per location
  • Set S9 clock to Melbourne time before Day 1 — metadata timestamps matter in edit
  • Confirm Penguin Parade booking — note: no cameras after sunset, strictly enforced
  • Check 12 Apostles drone clearance — Port Campbell National Park
  • Backup drive or high-capacity card — 7 days of 4K fills fast
  • Check Puffing Billy departure time — arrive early to scout track position
